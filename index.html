APOGEE NOTES

Field guide

Surface gravity
Measured in Earth gravities. At the same radius, stronger gravity means the satellite travels faster and finishes an orbit sooner.
Orbit radius
Measured from the planet’s center in planet radii (Rₚ). Farther out, the satellite travels more slowly and has more distance to cover.
Inclination
Tilts the satellite’s orbit relative to the planet’s equatorial plane. It changes the path’s orientation while leaving the orbital speed and period unchanged.

Apogee models circular orbits around a planet with a radius of 24,000 km. The illustration compresses distances to fit on screen and accelerates time by 3,600×.
